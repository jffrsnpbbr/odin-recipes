Sisig

Sisig is a crunchy, savory appetizer or main dish often served on a sizzling plate

Ingredients

  • 1 kg pork belly (liempo) or a mix of pig ears and snout.
  • 1/4 kg chicken liver (chopped) or liver spread.
  • 2 large white or red onions (finely minced) and 1 thumb of ginger (minced)
  • Soy sauce or liquid seasoning, calamansi juice, and salt/pepper.
  • 3–5 pieces of green and red chili peppers (siling labuyo).
  • Mayonnaise (for creaminess) and an egg for topping

Steps

  1. Boil the pork in water with salt and peppercorns until tender, then drain and let it air-dry.
  2. Grill the pork until charred or deep-fry it until the skin is very crispy.
  3. Coarsely chop the crispy pork into very small bits.
  4. In a separate pan, sauté the chicken liver until fully cooked, then mash or chop it finely.
  5. Mix the chopped pork, liver, onions, ginger, and chilies in a bowl or hot pan.
  6. Add the liquid seasoning, calamansi juice, and mayonnaise (if using). Toss until well-blended.
  7. For the best experience, serve on a pre-heated sizzling plate and top with a raw egg while it's still popping.
